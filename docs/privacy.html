Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · LYRA corp / Meta-Forge

This Privacy Policy explains how Meta-Forge handles information when you use the Meta-Forge website and Android app.

Information We Handle

  • Local app data: Meta-Forge stores mod files, imported files, and app preferences locally on your device.
  • Device/app permissions: The app may request storage/file access, network access, package visibility for detecting supported installed games, and install/download-related permissions needed for its features.
  • External links/downloads: When you open Discord, source pages, or external downloads, those third-party services may collect data under their own policies.

What We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Meta-Forge does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information through the static website. The app is designed to operate primarily on-device. If you contact LYRA corp through Discord or another third-party platform, that platform handles your information according to its own policy.

Third-Party Services

Meta-Forge may link to or download from third-party services such as GitHub, Discord, and mod source websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Review them before using those services.

Children's Privacy

Meta-Forge is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party support channel, contact the relevant platform or LYRA corp through Discord.

Security

We use reasonable design choices to keep the app simple and local-first, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. Only download and install files from sources you trust.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as Meta-Forge changes. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact LYRA corp via Discord: https://discord.gg/ydSPJUVMb2.
